## 1. Executive Summary & Core Scientific Thesis

This document provides a comprehensive mathematical and empirical audit of QuantEngine's maker execution model, queue-depletion logic, and fill-probability formulation across:
- `core/execution/maker_execution_model.py`
- `core/paper_trading/queue_aware_paper_engine.py`
- `core/research/queue_dynamics.py`
- `core/research/queue_calibration_engine.py`

### Fundamental Finding:
**A continuous Level 2 (L2) market-data capture—regardless of whether it spans 120 seconds or 24 hours—does not and cannot identify the queue-fill parameters of a resting maker order.**

Passive order-book depth updates observe only the aggregate depth $S(p, t)$ and net changes $\Delta S(p, t)$. They cannot observe the order's relative queue priority $\theta \in [0, 1]$ or the proportion of cancellations occurring ahead of the order $C_{ahead}(t)$. 

Without authentic order-level fill labels from the exchange matching engine, any parameter fit is degenerate and unidentifiable. The model status must remain strictly **`UNCALIBRATED / PARTIALLY_OBSERVABLE`**.

## 2. Seven-Category Epistemological Taxonomy

To separate observable market facts from simulation assumptions and prevent circular validation, all components are classified into seven non-overlapping categories:

```mermaid

```

### Category 1: Direct Market-Data Observations
Observable directly from authenticated venue feeds without model assumptions:
- **Best Bid Price ($bb_t$)**: Top bid price level on exchange order book.
- **Best Ask Price ($ba_t$)**: Top ask price level on exchange order book.
- **Displayed Level Depth ($S(p, t)$)**: Aggregate quantity displayed at price level $p$.
- **Exchange Event Timestamp ($t_{venue}$)**: Microsecond timestamp generated by matching engine clock.
- **Venue Public Trade Prints ($V_{trade}(p, t)$)**: Individual executed trade quantities from `all_trades` stream.

### Category 2: Derived Book Features
Deterministically computed from Category 1 without statistical fitting:
- **Top-of-Book Spread**: $\text{Spread}_t = ba_t - bb_t$.
- **Net Depth Delta**: $\Delta S(p, \Delta t) = S(p, t + \Delta t) - S(p, t)$.
- **Receive Transit Latency**: $L_t = t_{receipt, UTC} - t_{venue, UTC}$.
- **Empirical Depth Turnover**: $\tau_{depth} = \frac{1}{N} \sum |\Delta S(p, \Delta t)| / \Delta t$.

### Category 3: Hypothetical Queue States
Constructed states for a hypothetical order that does not physically exist in the venue's order book:
- **Hypothetical Entry Timestamp ($t_0$)**: Monotonic timestamp when simulation assumes order reached engine.
- **Assumed Queue Ahead ($Q_{ahead}(t_0)$)**: Displayed volume assumed to be resting ahead of our hypothetical order:
  $$Q_{ahead}(t_0) = \theta \cdot S(p, t_0), \quad \theta \in [0, 1]$$
- **Remaining Simulated Queue ($Q_{ahead}(t)$)**: Simulated depletion remaining before a hypothetical fill.

### Category 4: Model-Generated Predictions
Analytical or statistical outputs produced by mathematical models:
- **Poisson Fill Probability ($P_{fill}(H)$)**:
  $$P_{fill}(H) = 1 - \exp\left(-\frac{V_{eff} \cdot H}{Q_{ahead} + \text{size}}\right)$$
- **Expected Time to Fill ($\mathbb{E}[T_{fill}]$)**:
  $$\mathbb{E}[T_{fill}] = \frac{Q_{ahead}}{V_{eff}}$$
- **Adverse Selection Drag Estimate**:
  $$\text{Cost}_{USD} = \text{Notional} \cdot \frac{\delta_{bps}}{10,000}$$

### Category 5: Actual Venue Order Acknowledgements & Fills
Empirical ground truth from live exchange matching engine interactions:
- **Client Order ID ($\text{cl\_ord\_id}$)**: Unique identifier generated by client.
- **Exchange Order ID ($\text{ord\_id}$)**: Authoritative identifier assigned by matching engine.
- **Order Acknowledgement Timestamp ($t_{ack}$)**: Venue engine timestamp confirming order is resting.
- **Fill Record ($fill\_id, p_{fill}, q_{fill}, t_{fill}$)**: Matching engine fill trade record from `v2/user_trades`.
- **Cancellation Confirmation ($t_{cancel}$)**: Terminal state confirmation from engine.
- *Status in Phase 187/188: EXACTLY ZERO (`writes_to_exchange = 0`).*

### Category 6: Empirically Estimated Parameters
Parameters identified and estimated from paired Category 1 and Category 5 observations:
- *Status in Phase 188: **NONE / VACUOUS**.*
- No empirical parameter estimation is mathematically possible without Category 5 venue execution records.

### Category 7: Uncalibrated Assumptions
Heuristics, fixed rules, and policy defaults currently used in the codebase:

## 4. Mathematical Demonstration of Underdetermination

Consider an order resting at price $p$ on the bid:
$$\Delta S(p, \Delta t) = A_{new}(p, \Delta t) - V_{trades}(p, \Delta t) - C_{ahead}(\Delta t) - C_{behind}(\Delta t)$$

The true fill condition for the maker order is:
$$\int_0^T V_{trade, aggressor}(\tau) d\tau + C_{ahead}(T) \ge \theta \cdot S(p, 0)$$

### Degenerate Solution Space:
Let $\Delta S(p, \Delta t) = -500\text{ contracts}$ and $V_{trades} = 100\text{ contracts}$.
Total cancellations are:
$$C_{total} = -\Delta S - V_{trades} = 500 - 100 = 400\text{ contracts}$$

Because passive L2 feeds report only $S(p, t)$:
1. If $\theta = 0.90$ (order is in the tail, $Q_{ahead} = 900$) and $C_{ahead} = 0$, effective depletion is $100$ contracts $\rightarrow$ **ORDER DOES NOT FILL**.
2. If $\theta = 0.10$ (order is in the front, $Q_{ahead} = 100$) and $C_{ahead} = 0$, effective depletion is $100$ contracts $\rightarrow$ **ORDER FILLS**.
3. If $\theta = 0.50$ ($Q_{ahead} = 500$) and all cancellations occurred ahead ($C_{ahead} = 400$), effective depletion is $100 + 400 = 500$ contracts $\rightarrow$ **ORDER FILLS**.
4. If $\theta = 0.50$ ($Q_{ahead} = 500$) and all cancellations occurred behind ($C_{ahead} = 0$), effective depletion is $100$ contracts $\rightarrow$ **ORDER DOES NOT FILL**.

All four combinations produce the **exact same observable L2 market-data sequence**. Therefore:
$$\Pr(\text{Fill} \mid \text{Passive L2 Data}) \text{ is mathematically unidentifiable.}$$
